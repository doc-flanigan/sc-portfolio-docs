Latest official word (Q&A: Anvil Aerospace F8C Lightning, comm-link 19512, 2023-10-11): "Those who play Squadron 42 may earn a number of bonuses that carry over into Star Citizen, including access to the F8C Lightning." The Q&A carries CIG's standard disclaimer that answers reflect development intentions at the time of writing.

Original description (Reverse the Verse LIVE: Squadron 42 Q&A, 2018-01-26, from 9:08): Chris Roberts says a few ships are tied to Squadron 42 progress. Reach the point in the campaign where you fly one and you earn a badge that lets you get that ship in the persistent universe; the F8C requires playing near the end of the campaign, and the F7A Hornet is named as another. He describes it as enabling you to purchase the ship in the PU, so the reward is access, not a free ship. He also says story progress and character relationships can unlock missions and content in the PU, and that unlocks track how far you have progressed.

Scope: the rewards are for the persistent universe (the live game) on your Star Citizen account. The PTU test server copies ships and items from your Live account, so it is not a separate place to earn them. Squadron 42 is not yet released, so none of this is in the game today and the details can change. The F8C was also sold for a limited time during an in-game event in 2023 (same Q&A), separately from the Squadron 42 route.
