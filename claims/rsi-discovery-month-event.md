Exact lines (CIG Spectrum announcement "Alpha 4.10.2 - RSI Discovery Month", 2026-10-09):
"RSI is celebrating the arrival of the next-generation Constellation, the new Mk V Centaurus,
and all pilots across Stanton are invited to join in." / "Defend fellow pilots from the
criminals... keep vital supplies moving across Stanton, or collect industrial materials and
lost cargo." Reward tracks: "Ship paints, flight blades, components, armor, and weapons".

**It is NOT a Free Fly.** No free-account window, no trial. Open to "every pilot" — any
account that owns a game package. freeflyevent only links to it as a free *ship rental*.

**End date is NOT in the official text we read.** A third-party referral site claims an
Oct 20, 2026 close citing CIG's FAQ, but FAQ 21350 as read on 2026-10-09 does not contain it.
Do not publish an end date until an official line is found. Related dated facts that ARE
official: feedback survey open until Oct 31 (Spectrum feedback thread); Constellation Mk IV
retirement window "will end on January 27, 2027" (FAQ 21350); the RSI Galaxy thematic paint
from the goodies pack "will be delivered alongside the ship itself when it arrives at this
year's Intergalactic Aerospace Expo" (FAQ 21350) — i.e. the event precedes IAE 2956.
