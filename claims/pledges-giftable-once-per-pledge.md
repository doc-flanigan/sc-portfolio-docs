Added 2026-10-02; reworded 2026-10-29 for the October 2026 gifting change.

Exact lines:
- RSI Knowledge Base, Gifting FAQs (edited 2026-10-02): "Game packages, standalone ships, and other pledge store items may be gifted once. This is counted as once per pledge, not once per account." Under "What you can't gift": "Your protected game access pledges."
- CIG Spectrum, "Updates to Pledge Gifting and Game Access" (2026-08-27): "The following changes will apply only to pledges purchased after the new gifting system takes effect in October. Existing pledges will retain their current gifting functionality. Pledges intended as gifts must be designated as such at the time of purchase. A pledge designated as a gift cannot be used by the purchaser." Same post: the primary Game Access pledge "will become permanently associated with the account and cannot be melted or gifted."
- CIG Spectrum, "Updates to Pledge Gifting - FAQ" (2026-10-27): "The updates to gifting will take effect on October 30." / "Can I gift anything? Only items where the 'Buying as a gift?' option appears at checkout." / "Existing hangar gifts and gift codes are grandfathered in."

The KB article predates the October 30 change. Re-check it once RSI updates it, and drop the "before October 30" framing when the old rule no longer matters.
