Added 2026-10-02. Re-verified 2026-10-29 against the October 2026 gifting change: still accurate, because the new rules apply to every recipient alike.

What those restrictions now are (exact lines in the `pledges-giftable-once-per-pledge` entry):
- From October 30, 2026, a new pledge can only be gifted if it is marked as a gift at checkout, and a gift pledge can't be used by the buyer — so to put a new pledge on a second account, buy it as a gift and claim it there.
- Pledges bought before October 30 keep the old rule: gifted once per pledge.
- An account's protected Game Access pledge can't be gifted at all, to another account or anyone else (KB "What you can't gift"; Spectrum 2026-08-27).
