Verified 2026-11-02 (sc-fact-check). Both articles showed updated_at 2026-07-29; the support
site 403s WebFetch, so they were read through the public Zendesk Help Center JSON API on the
same domain.

- Taxes article: VAT is listed for "EU, UK, Norway, South Africa, Russia, New Zealand,
  Switzerland, South Korea, and Japan"; "The tax will be figured based on the billing address
  associated with your payment method."
- Orders FAQ: "Depending on your selected store region, taxes may or may not already be
  factored into any pledge store prices until billing information is entered during checkout."

Do NOT say VAT is always added on top of the listed price: RSI says it depends on store
region. Store currency (USD vs EUR/GBP) is unverifiable from official sources; don't claim it.
