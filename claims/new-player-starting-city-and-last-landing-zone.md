Added 2026-10-18. RSI KB "Getting Started in the 'Verse" (updated 2026-07-27): "The first time you load into the Stanton System from the Universe menu, you will get to choose to spawn in one of several major city locations throughout the 'verse: New Babbage, Area18, Lorville, or Orison. Choose carefully - from then on you will wake up at the last landing zone you flew to that has hab facilities." 4.10.2 global.ini (Frontend_PU_HomeBaseExplained1) adds that the chosen location is also the primary residence where ships and items are stored.
