Comm-link 21341 (published 2026-10-12): "From October 14 to 27 … watching any Drops-enabled Star
Citizen stream for a total of four hours to unlock the Phoenix Insignia Display for free." The FAQ
says no Amazon Luna or Prime Gaming is needed, and one Twitch account maps to one RSI account. 21374
(This Week in Star Citizen, Oct 12) repeats the dates. CAUTION: 21341 says "Starting October 14,
with the launch of RSI Discovery Month". The event itself began with 4.10.2 on Oct 9, so don't
restate that line. The drops window closing on Oct 27 is NOT a Discovery Month end date.
