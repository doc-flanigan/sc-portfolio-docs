Verified 2026-11-06 on RSI's rendered Terms of Service (U.S. version), header "LAST UPDATED AND EFFECTIVE AS OF OCTOBER 30, 2026", Section V. Exact lines: "Any Virtual Goods rewards for referrals will only remain credited to your account if the referral transaction remains valid for 30 days." / "A valid referral for this purpose is a valid account established by a new player with a value of at least $40USD for at least 30 days."

The ToS does not say what "value" counts or how the 30 days are measured. Do not claim more. Related: [[referral-recruitment-point-40-usd]], [[gifts-allowed-to-referred-players]].
