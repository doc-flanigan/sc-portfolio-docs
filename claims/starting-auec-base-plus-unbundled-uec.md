Added 2026-10-18 (dayone 4.10.2 accuracy pass). RSI KB "Currencies of Star Citizen (UEC/aUEC/REC/Store Credit)" (updated 2026-08-06): "For any given patch, if you have no current aUEC total to carry over, your starting aUEC will contain a base starting amount determined by testing needs, plus all UEC on your account that is not bundled with a game package." And: "the Duelist Starter Pack includes 10000 UEC, which will not be added to your starting aUEC total during testing."

Why it matters: this explains why the 50,000 UEC referral bonus counts toward starting aUEC (see referral-enlistment-bonus-50k-uec.md) while a starter pack's bundled 10,000 UEC does not (see citizen-starter-pack-10000-uec-6mo-insurance.md). The base amount is per patch; 20,000 as of Alpha 4.10.x (new-character-starting-auec-20000.md). The same article says aUEC is temporary and balances can reset.
